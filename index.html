Engineering Capabilities

Our Core Validation Domains

Comprehensive qualification solutions tailored to sterile, oral solid dosage, and biotechnology manufacturing plants.

HVAC & Cleanroom Environmental Validation

Complete qualification and certification of HVAC systems and controlled environments in compliance with ISO 14644 and cGMP regulations.

  • Air Balancing & Airflow Measurement (Capture Hoods & Anemometers)
  • HEPA Filter Integrity Testing (Aerosol Photometer & Generator)
  • Room Differential Pressure Balancing & Room Clean-up Recovery
  • Airborne Particle Counts (ISO 14644-1 Classes 5 through 8)
  • Airflow Pattern Visualization (Smoke Studies with Video Documentation)

Thermal Validation & Mapping

Precise thermal qualification of critical sterilization equipment and temperature/humidity mapping of controlled storage zones.

  • Autoclaves & Super-Heated Water Sterilizers
  • Dry Heat Sterilizers & Depyrogenation Tunnels
  • Cold Storage Rooms (2–8°C) & Deep Freezers (-20°C)
  • Systematic Warehouse Temperature Mapping
